StillWave

Design v3 — a fresh start

Rebuilt from zero. Only the logo survives; the palette, type, components, navigation, artwork and layouts are all new. Every feature’s landing screen is shown for Fire TV (960×540 dp, rendered 2× for 1080p) and phone landscape (800×360 dp).

What’s different, and why. Apple’s materials and focus guidance drives the foundation: translucent glass exists only in the floating control layer; content sits on flat tinted surfaces; focus lifts and brightens the item instead of drawing outlines. Artwork is generative colour-field “mesh” art, not fake photographs — honest placeholder for real channel art. All names and numbers are invented sample content.

Foundation

Two layers: a content layer (full-bleed art, flat tinted surfaces) and a functional layer (navigation, transport, sheets) in glass that floats above it. Colour comes from the content; the UI itself is nearly monochrome with one brand accent taken from the logo.

Home

Live first. One featured live item with progress and the action order Watch · Start over · Record · Favorite, then a shelf of what’s on now. The artwork carries the colour; the glass pill is the only chrome.

Sports Center

Cards, not rows. The live game gets the largest card; everything else is sized by importance. Watch appears only when the guide carries the channel; otherwise the card says so quietly.

Radio & Podcasts

Shelves of covers. Continue first, then stations, with a persistent glass now-playing bar. The whole page tints to the playing cover.

Daily Dashboard

A bento of unequal tiles. Weather is the lead; the astronomy picture is the visual anchor; headlines and games are quiet.

Local Media (phone only)

One action. Empty state explains the privacy boundary; recents are thumbnails with duration and resume progress; broken files say why.

Live World (Fire TV only)

Place and time are the content. Each feed shows its local time and temperature; the backdrop is the focused feed.

Ambient Mode (Fire TV only)

The scene is the interface. Slow-moving colour fields (animated here), no controls once running, a quiet clock.

Multiview

Two clear states. Mint outline + speaker chip = which picture has sound. White lift = D-pad focus. A single glass control capsule below.

Casting and handoff

A glass sheet over the playing picture. Fire TV screens first, Google Cast second, unusable screens explained. Focused row = opaque highlight.
